The House of Pawlish

A quieter, more considered kind of grooming.

Pawlish was built for pet parents who expect more. Every visit is booked with time to spare, so your companion is never rushed, never crated for hours, and always handled by a groomer who knows them by name.

  • i.

    One pet, one groomer

    Your pet stays with the same hands from first brush to final spritz.

  • ii.

    Salon-grade products

    Gentle, pH-balanced shampoos and conditioners chosen for each coat type.

  • iii.

    Calm by design

    Low-stress handling, quiet dryers and plenty of treats along the way.
