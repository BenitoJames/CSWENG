The House of Pawlish

A quieter, more considered kind of grooming.

Pawlish was built for pet parents who expect more. Like a visit to your own favourite spa, every groom is personal, unhurried and spotlessly clean, so your companion leaves looking and smelling their very best.

  • i.

    Your choice of scent

    Pick the shampoo and cologne your pet goes home wearing.

  • ii.

    Nothing shared

    Towels and toothbrushes are never shared between pets. Hygiene comes first.

  • iii.

    Calm by design

    Gentle, patient handling for pets who usually find grooming stressful.

The Experience

From arrival to the reveal.

  1. Consultation

    We start with a conversation about your pet's coat, temperament and the look you have in mind.

  2. Spa bath

    A warm, unhurried bath with the shampoo scent you chose, using their own fresh towels.

  3. Sculpting

    Precise cuts and hand-scissored detail, finished with patience rather than speed.

  4. The reveal

    A spritz of their chosen cologne, a portrait on our photo set, and a pet ready to go home.
